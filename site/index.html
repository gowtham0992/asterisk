A result you can question

“No overlap”
needs a definition.

In the published WSI-Bench report-task files, Asterisk measured 42 patient identifiers shared between training and evaluation, affecting 43 of 208 evaluation rows. A slide-only check misses that distinction.

This is a saved local measurement of pinned public files, not a live model response or a new Sandbox run. Shared patients matter when the claim requires unseen patients; they do not, by themselves, quantify an effect on model accuracy.

What the measurement establishes

Exact identifier overlap under the recorded derivations. It does not establish duplicate image content, preprocessing leakage, a performance penalty, or independence at an undocumented level. The downloadable JSON retains source provenance and witness rows.

Research preview

A precise check.
An explicit boundary.

Asterisk checks recorded identities, not whether an entire experiment is scientifically valid. Zero overlap stays pending when the identity meaning needs confirmation.

In 41 evaluated cases across tested configurations, guarded model runs produced no false reassurance. Nemotron Super proposed 21 ladders correctly; the Sonnet comparison proposed 36. The shipping Super v4 prompt scored 5/10 on set D and failed its preparation gate. A later denial control elicited an unsupported binding. These results do not guarantee reliability on new datasets. Researcher usefulness remains unmeasured.
