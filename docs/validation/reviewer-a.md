Your criticism is useful; there is no expectation of a positive verdict.

Record any factual contradiction or unsupported definitive.

Commit the completed ratings file with a timestamp before we reveal expected outcomes. We require zero unsupported definitive conclusions and a witness reproduced without builder help within fifteen minutes. Repeatability is not scientific validity. A pending confirmation is not a clear verdict. Do not execute supplied project Python; use isolated replay or independently inspect the CSV rows.
