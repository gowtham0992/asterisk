One fresh public action-recognition dataset, official recognition split 1. Freeze the independent regex-based reference, hashes, claim and upload inventory before inference. Keep original split lists unchanged; no videos, audio, demographic or biometric fields. Researcher-authored setup notes cite the official dataset page and paper; this is not a blinded usability study.

Run shipping v4 Nemotron preparation through the product UI, at most two provider calls (the normal single validation repair) and one Sandbox replay. Estimated development spend cap $0.25; do not use the hosted judge reserve. Keep original proposal and failures; manual corrections do not become first-pass successes. Never retry inference just to improve the result.

Success checks: both source lists included with correct partitions, action-qualified groups derived with grouping cardinalities equal to the independent reference, no unsupported actor claim, 100% mapping coverage, meaning explicitly reviewed, receipt retaining source/derivation/citation provenance, Sandbox recomputation matching, and evidence ZIP recomputed from a separate trusted installation. Report local-only previews as previews and human confirmations as agent-operated simulations.

Report separately: first-pass proposal validity and correctness, provider repair count, necessary corrections, optional correction exercise, latency, tokens and conservative estimated spend; every incomplete stage. The raw lists are TXT rather than CSV, so record file-role selection friction. Setup notes are authored by the developer and contain explicit naming/partition guidance: this tests end-to-end handling, not unaided discovery.
