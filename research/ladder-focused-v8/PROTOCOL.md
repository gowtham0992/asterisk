# Focused preparation v8 — fixed development test

Freeze before inference. Compare shipping v4 and a distinct v8 candidate: remove the broad worked example, request only observation plus the claimed unit, and give source samples a separate grammar. The trusted engine, payload and validator are identical in both arms. This is implementer-authored development evidence, not independent human evidence or a held-out accuracy claim.

Twelve original fictional cases, two trials in each arm: 48 proposals, at most 96 inference calls including the existing one repair. Eight supported cases cover action-qualified source groups, middle characters, lookup distractors, per-file column overrides, excluded partition labels, compound namespace units, observation claims, and per-sample ID traps. Four controls lack a generating-unit mapping, deny a binding, require an unsupported noncontiguous derivation, or omit assignment semantics. No real research data, personal data, Sandbox calls or judge allowance. Exact requests, expected complete sample/group/partition maps, source hashes and implementation bytes are retained privately before inference. Labels must never be edited after running.

Maximum estimated development spend $3.00, using deliberately conservative $10/million input and output reservation rates (an estimate, not a quoted provider price). Maximum 8,000 completion tokens per call, no SDK retries, at most one validator repair, two concurrent proposals. Provider failures retain their reservations. No post-result retry or second batch. A partially executed study is inconclusive. Final diagnostics and raw first replies survive.

Promotion requires all 24 candidate proposals ultimately semantically correct, all eight control attempts abstaining, no paired final regression, at least two more first-call semantically correct attempts than v4, no wrong candidate binding, no provider failure, and the same default model. Structural validity and success on a single demo are not correctness. A supported-case abstention is a failure. Compare every sample's grouping and every sample's partition, including excluded rows; a correct overlap count cannot rescue a wrong binding. Any failed criterion means the candidate stays research-only. Passing authorizes a new accurately labeled development default, not a claim of universal accuracy or replacement of the historical 5/10 score.

Human usefulness is a separate gate: actual screened participants must read the frozen receipt uncoached. This test cannot close it.
