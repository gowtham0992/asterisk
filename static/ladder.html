What a ladder
can tell you.

At each level, do training and evaluation share a recorded value? That is what is measured, from exact identifiers under the derivations the spec states and the documentation it cites.

“Held out” stays pending until a person confirms what the level means: a column called patient_id can still hold image identifiers. Content similarity, preprocessing and the effect on accuracy are not measured.
