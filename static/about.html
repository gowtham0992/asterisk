Different samples. Same subjects?

Training on one image of a specimen and evaluating on another image of the same specimen does not test performance on unseen specimens. Asterisk records an exact-identity overlap check, with the assumptions and evidence needed to inspect it.

One claim, one binding

Start a split receipt. Supply a CSV split, an identity mapping and documentation. State the generating unit your evaluation should hold out. NVIDIA Nemotron Super reads previews through Nebius Token Factory and proposes the columns and labels. You see cardinalities computed from all rows, correct the fields, and confirm their meaning before execution.

Repeated or blank sample identifiers, identical sample and partition columns, absent labels and conflicting mappings are rejected with specific errors. These are binding checks, not proof that an entity column means “patient” or “specimen.” That assumption remains yours.

What the receipt establishes

After upload authorization, the confirmed arguments go to a trusted checker in Nebius Token Factory Sandboxes. The checker computes overlap and mapping coverage, verifies witness rows, and repeats the same measurement in a fresh Sandbox. A replay mismatch suppresses the verdict.

  • Violation: recorded identities occur on both sides of the confirmed split.
  • Clear: no recorded identity overlaps, coverage is complete, and the exact binding has a recorded confirmation. This is not experiment certification.
  • Insufficient: the available mapping or binding does not establish separation.
  • Incomplete: execution or replay failed; no definitive result is available.

Factual findings and missing-evidence text come from the harness. Any model interpretation is separately labeled unverified. Identity assumptions, undocumented links, duplicate content and accuracy effects are outside the check.

A receipt someone else can inspect

Export the claim, input files, confirmed binding, source quotations, checker source, measured results and replay records. The included verifier checks retained file hashes. Hashes show byte integrity relative to an unsigned manifest; replay shows repeatability. Neither establishes scientific validity or independent provenance.

Scope and evaluation

Only entity overlap is offered. Checkpoint and preprocessing code remain research artifacts. V3 acceptance failed; v4 is a new development iteration. Human evidence review, comprehension and evidence of reduced verification effort remain unmeasured. A synthetic example or automated confirmation is not a human study.

Your data

Records belong to this browser session and are retained in the local application database. Planning sends selected previews and column statistics to Nebius; execution sends the listed files to Sandboxes after authorization. Use only data you may share; do not submit personal or sensitive data during the beta. Deletion from the active database does not erase backups or provider records.
