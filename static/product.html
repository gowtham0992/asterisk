One identifier check can pass while the level your claim depends on leaks. Asterisk measures recorded overlap at declared levels—image, recording, patient, site—and marks the one your claim needs.

  1. Nemotron proposes the levels from your documentation, on Nebius Token Factory, citing the lines it used.
  2. ✱Asterisk measures each supported level on the app server, with guards against specific misleading bindings.
  3. A Nebius Sandbox recomputes the result from the engine's own code when you request replay. Local results can also be saved.

A split check is a join once the identity and partitions are known. The receipt records those choices, the rows behind the result and the derivation rules used to measure it.

This is a reproduction of a known finding, not a new discovery. It establishes lesion overlap—not patient overlap, an accuracy loss or researcher benefit.

From claim to receipt

One check.
Every assumption visible.

  1. 01

    Bring the split.

    Add your dataset’s supported CSV files and documentation. State what should be unseen: patients, subjects or specimens. Preview exactly what the model would receive.

  2. 02

    Review the meaning.

    Nemotron proposes extraction rules with source quotations. Inspect the rules and partition counts. If it cannot prepare them, use the guided definition form.

  3. 03

    Keep the evidence.

    Application code measures overlap on the app server. Confirm the identity meaning before a zero-overlap result reads held out. Request one optional fresh Sandbox replay, then save and export the receipt.

The model has a specific job

Read the definition.
Propose the rule.

A join can measure overlap once the identifiers are ready. Nemotron helps interpret the dataset’s documentation and proposes supported rules such as taking a patient prefix from a slide filename.

You can inspect and edit that proposal. Application code computes the measurements; model prose cannot supply a verdict.

Reliability is still bounded

Useful evidence.
No blanket guarantee.

The shipping Nemotron Super prompt scored 5 of 10 cases on its retained set D and failed the preparation gate. The guided definition form is available when preparation is incomplete.

Recorded guards prevented false reassurance in the retained evaluation sets. A later denial control elicited an unsupported binding; review remains necessary. Researcher usefulness remains unmeasured.

What this check
can tell you.

Do the selected training and evaluation rows share recorded identities? That is the question Asterisk answers.

No overlap is conditional on complete mapping coverage and the identity binding you confirm. Missing evidence stays visible.

This check does not detect duplicate content, undocumented identity links, preprocessing leakage or changes in model accuracy. A clear receipt does not certify an experiment.
