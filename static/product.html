From claim to receipt

One check.
Every assumption visible.

  1. 01

    Bring the split.

    Add your CSV split, identity mapping and documentation. State what should be absent from training: patients, subjects or specimens.

  2. 02

    Review the meaning.

    Nemotron proposes the columns and labels. Inspect their counts, correct the fields and confirm what the identity represents.

  3. 03

    Keep the evidence.

    A trusted checker measures overlap in a Nebius Sandbox. A fresh Sandbox repeats it. Inspect the witness rows, compare a revised split and export the receipt.

After the finding

Review a repair.
Keep both receipts.

Export every affected row. Preview which records would change partitions, inspect the new sizes, then approve a separate revision. The original evidence stays intact.

Bring realized arrays from a notebook or reuse saved columns from the command line. A new split always needs a new check.

A measured example

Does the score change?

In a controlled experiment on real repeated recordings, removing planted participant overlap increased prediction error by 0.117 points on average across five fixed seeds.

A size-matched control changed error by 0.004 points. All runs are reported; the control is a disclosed post-review extension. This does not establish researcher benefit or a general effect size.

What this check
can tell you.

Do the selected training and evaluation rows share recorded identities? That is the question Asterisk answers.

No overlap is conditional on complete mapping coverage and the identity binding you confirm. Missing evidence stays visible.

This check does not detect duplicate content, undocumented identity links, preprocessing leakage or changes in model accuracy. A clear receipt does not certify an experiment.
