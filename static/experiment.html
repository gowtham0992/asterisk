What changed
when the split changed?

Prediction error rose by 0.117 points on average after removing planted participant overlap.

A 1.60% increase relative to mean overlapping error. Removing the same number of other training rows changed error by only 0.004 points on average.

The same recordings.
A different training boundary.

We used 5,875 recordings from 42 participants in the public Parkinsons Telemonitoring dataset. Six held-out participants deliberately had some recordings placed in training. Asterisk’s repair moved those recordings back into evaluation.

For each seed, all three fitted models were scored on exactly the same evaluation rows. Shared participant IDs fell from six to zero.

What this establishes.

We can trace a proposed partition change through the recorded identities and rerun a fixed predictive experiment. The error rose in all five repair runs. The size-matched control removes the same number of rows from non-evaluation participants while retaining the leaked recordings. The smaller control change supports a leakage-related explanation beyond training count alone.

What it does not.

This is a planted condition, not a newly discovered defect in published research. The control matches training size, but not every participant or feature characteristic. These five fixed draws do not establish a general causal effect, statistical significance, clinical importance, or researcher benefit. This dataset was previously used in development.

Recorded identity separation does not certify the experiment or provide clinical validation.

Reproduce the measurement.

Post-review extension: we added the size-matched control after Fable supplied its results. It is not preregistered or unseen. The original protocol and results are preserved; docs/experiments/size-control-protocol.md records the amendment.

The source release includes scripts/run_consequence_experiment.py and the protocol in docs/experiments/score-protocol.md. Download the source archive from UCI into .data/probe-pilot/resources/parkinsons.zip, then run:

.venv/bin/python scripts/run_consequence_experiment.py .data/consequence-experiment-reproduction

The output retains split files, exact row moves, control removals, configurations, fitted coefficients, hashes and a manifest. It runs locally without inference or data uploads.
