The participant is identified by the first three barcode components, Project-TSS-Participant, for example TCGA-02-0001 (12 characters).
TSS is the tissue source site, for example 02; TCGA-02 (7 characters) therefore names the project prefix and site.
"A parent barcode prefixes any of its descendent barcodes, reflecting the derivation of one biospecimen type from another."
